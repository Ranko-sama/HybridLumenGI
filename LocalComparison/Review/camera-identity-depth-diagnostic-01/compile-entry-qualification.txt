Its semantic compiler arguments follow Tests/Validate-ReceiverIdentityShaders.ps1

UNITY_VERSION=600000, SHADER_TARGET=50; DEBUG_DISPLAY=1 for the debug fragment.
-H, -MD and -MF only retain include/dependency evidence. It compiles the three
distinct HLSL entries once; three ShaderLab Cull states share those entry bodies.

files, original vertex, original strict depth gate and original candidate CAS
