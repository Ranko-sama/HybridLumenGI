Camera motion and real URP TAA: finite prospective contract, version 1.

Purpose: independently test f-to-f-1 correspondence and first-exposure rejection
under actual URP camera motion and nonzero jitter. This is not an integral/RGB
reference, arbitrary camera-projection certificate, or overall image-quality test.
No product shader arithmetic changes. No Unity execution until root schedules it.

Frozen scene: finite wall x=0,y=[0,2],z=[-2,2], +X; floor y=0,x=[0,5],
z=[-3,3], +Y; fixed black panel z=-.2,x=[.4,1.8],y=[0,1.8], -Z.
Each is one charted opaque URP Lit rectangle (two triangles), metallic0/no maps.
MPB shader-linear wall(.5,.25,.125,1), floor(1,1,1,1), panel(0,0,0,1).
White directional toLight +X intensity1/shadowsNone; no other lights or environment.
All three source transforms/materials/light remain fixed throughout the sequence.

Exactly 48 consecutive full URP StandardRequest renders, disabled automatic camera;
one request per consecutive engine frame. Deadline 300 seconds; no added warmups.
Frames0..31 camera(3,3,-3), initial rotation LookAt(1,.4,0); frames32..39
camera.x=3+.075*(logical-31), y=3,z=-3; frames40..47 x=3.6. Rotation remains
the initial quaternion, FOV50, near.05/far30, aspect640/360, full GI640x360.
Real URP camera TAA: post processing enabled, TemporalAntiAliasing/High,
jitterScale1, no MSAA/dynamic resolution/stack. Observe actual jitter delta/motion;
configured settings alone do not qualify TAA. GI temporal=true, maxHistory32,
filter0, receiverSourceTracking=true, receiverObjectHistory=true. RPP8,bias.03,
traceDistance20, guiding/feedback/screenSteps/emissive/area/environment all disabled.

No RGB, history length, or observed product validity result selects a pixel/cohort.

copy shader computes addresses only; independent analysis reconstructs UV enclosures
from actual matrices/depth/motion/jitter and verifies addresses and product branch.
Static source currentToPrevious must be identity and source key/candidate valid.
This contract qualifies the actual motionUV branch only: motionUV must provably
agree with static-pose projection within the product's one-full-pixel branch gate.
The pose fallback is not counted as motion-path coverage and is not certified here.
Nearest and all positive-weight bilinear taps need explicit independent support proof
for continued-history controls. Motion/projection disagreement or rounding-boundary
ambiguity is uncovered, not a radiance failure. No unsupported generalization from
the earlier fixed-camera helper. Use existing outward float32 interval primitives;
retain one-ULP outward rounding, D3D rsq relative2^-21 and sqrt bounds. Half radiance
