The four 256-record streams are a reasonable finite limit for the fixed workload and narrower recorder. Missing, stale, overflowed or unterminated streams fail explicitly; they do not trigger a capacity increase or another run. The cache start/terminal instrumentation also covers the original unscheduled-copy and uncovered paths, so absence of a ProxyRadiance call need not be mistaken for a missing dispatch. FrameEnded checks the terminal record's presence, not that the estimate was known. After the event it does not require positive raw alpha or history count one. Unknown output therefore remains distinguishable from measured black; the analysis must retain the raw validity and terminal-reason fields.

The shutdown correction addresses the last identified safety issue. Waiting for outstanding readbacks alone did not prove completion of observer UAV use on a non-detailed frame. Finish now closes admission and transfers the three buffer references to the existing resource-retirement mechanism in a finally block, including when evidence writing fails. Mode.Close independently restores MPB/mesh state and retires its owned dynamic mesh. The prepared material-mode Rendered catch records the failure and defers closing to the original next Update error branch, allowing the current render context to return before shutdown. The collector keeps the camera disabled instead of restoring an enabled camera for a tail frame. Partial configuration also invokes cleanup. These changes reuse the established ownership/fence rules; they do not assume WaitAllRequests proves all GPU work finished.

Result interpretation remains bounded. A generation increment, successful upload, actual evaluated hit and contribution to final raw GI are separate observations. Final consumption requires the original winner and nonzero weights plus a valid terminal estimate. A selected path that never consumes slot 0 is uncovered, not repairable by added rays. Slot-1 owner cards may recapture while slot-1 material identity remains unchanged. Target incoming GI need not turn blue, and other receivers may legitimately see changed lighting. Keep the original first-failure stop and conditional-fix-only replay limit; do not expand this into a material matrix or another performance investigation.
