## Event and coverage boundaries

Resolve the target through the existing probe's serialized `mutableRenderer` reference and verify the expected White receiver and slot layout. Preserve the slot-0 block by GetPropertyBlock into that same block before replacing only `_BaseColor`. A nonempty slot block replaces the entire renderer-level block, rather than inheriting its other entries (`HybridLumenGIMaterialSource.cs:205–231`). Stop preparation for the stated empty-slot/nonempty-renderer conflict; do not clear either block or merge guessed properties. Recheck the frozen input immediately before the one mutation. Preserve ordinary MPB source origin; pure 0/1 values avoid a new color-conversion experiment.

Disable the existing probe/sequence actions before they can start. The old material event explicitly calls MarkDirty and its neighboring frames trigger deformation, so reusing its Update wholesale would invalidate this question. Retain only the agreed initial deformation snapshot setup. Do not use newly observed color changes to choose pixels, cards or branches.

Freeze interior selections for slot 0, slot 1 and the independent renderer from pre-event geometry and actual identity. Full-resolution pixel centers and GI-selected sample centers must be distinguished. Multiple selected points may share one chart/cell; preserve that alias instead of overwriting one record or calling aliases independent cache samples. The frame-159 admission gate must name the actual receipts it uses and run before the edit. Pending detailed readbacks must not silently become successful gate evidence or cause extra rendering beyond the fixed logical timeline. Final frame 255 must receive its requested observations before the collector closes admission and drains its existing readbacks.

Reuse the existing safe command-buffer readback/copy placement so transient raw data and identity buffers are captured while alive. Do not read the post-frame `state.readIndex` and infer which resources an earlier callback used. Reserve completion responsibility before issuing requests; retain the established close/finish/error cleanup sequence. There is no need for a second query kernel or extra rays.

## Interpret only what is actually covered

SurfaceCache invalidates all cards of an owner on a material-source change (`HybridLumenGISurfaceCache.cs:467–510`). Slot 1 can therefore acquire new card generations and be recaptured even while its own material snapshot and receiver MaterialGeneration remain unchanged. That is the current invalidation granularity, not evidence that slot-0 MPB values leaked into slot 1. Record material content and receiver eligibility separately from owner-card scheduling.

For the target, the changed receiver MaterialGeneration should make SameSource/TryGetPrevious fail; `ReceiverIdentityFrame.Build` exposes that in the reusable flag. Actual current/previous identity and object-row reads establish what the temporal shader received. For a known, geometrically valid current measurement whose old history is rejected, the first resulting sample count is one. Unknown current data follows the existing unknown-history contract and must not be forced to measured black or sample count one. A retained zero RGB value can be a legitimate measured result; use alpha/validity rather than RGB alone. An unchanged source control can still have lighting-reactive history changes; do not require globally unchanged history counts or images.

The gather recorder already has hit identity and ProxyRadiance's actual diffuse/direct/cache/value fields. Join a target hit by full identity and context/call, then follow the original winner, nonzero sample/coefficient and valid terminal output. A queried hit that loses to screen visibility, a guide lookup, a zero-weight evaluation or an ultimately unknown estimate is not a contribution to final raw GI. If selected paths never consume slot 0, mark that branch uncovered; keep the proven source/binding/history results without moving pixels, increasing rays or rerunning.

The cache and raw receiver GI hold incoming illumination. Blue BaseColor is the input uniform; evaluated diffuse reflectance additionally includes the real texture, workflow and proxy tint, and ProxyRadiance applies its existing response. The target's incoming GI need not immediately turn blue, and unchanged neighboring materials can receive changed illumination. Final URP RGB also includes other lighting terms. Do not turn a visually blue image or a single generation increase into proof of the complete event chain, and do not require monotonic color or convergence by frame 255.

Proceed with this limited implementation. Preserve the declared first-failure stop and uncovered-branch reporting. Only a concrete product counterexample can justify the one conditional fixed replay; there is no reason here to add backend variants, another material operation, performance scopes or a general tracing framework.
